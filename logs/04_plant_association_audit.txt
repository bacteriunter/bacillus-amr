7. CURRENT OPERATIONAL DEFINITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Include only isolates whose metadata explicitly supports direct association with a plant, plant tissue/organ, plant surface, endosphere/endophyte, rhizoplane, or rhizosphere.
Agricultural soil, crop fields, compost, plant residues, leaf litter, foods, fermented products, and processed plant materials are not sufficient by themselves.
Plant organs such as leaf, root, stem, seed, flower, or nodule are acceptable when they represent the biological material from which the isolate was obtained.
Exclusion terms are interpreted in metadata context rather than applied globally across all fields.
No assembly is automatically excluded solely because another metadata field contains food- or fermentation-related terminology when the isolation source itself provides explicit direct plant association.
